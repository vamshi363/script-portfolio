MOVE 01 // "ATTACK & VISION" (THE DUH TEST + COMPARE) 0:00–0:05

Open With Instant Contrast & an Emotional Curiosity Gap

We compare two opposing realities in Frame 1 (Smart vs. Amateur approach) and pair a relatable character with a specific desired outcome—never an obvious "duh" statement.

MOVE 02 // "CONTROL OVER HERE" (BUT / THEREFORE CHAIN) Center Board

Replace "And Then" With "But / Therefore" + 5-Word Rhythm

Notice the pink box in the center of the chessboard? That's where viewers drop off if your sentences are all the same length. We chain every beat with "but" or "therefore" and drop ≤5-word punch lines between long sentences.

MOVE 03 // "CASTLING — GET THE KING SAFE" (THE DEATH ZONE) Everest Step 3

Level-3 Transformation Stakes & The "Death Zone" Pivot

Surface-level goals bore people. We take the viewer into the Death Zone—what the struggle actually cost—before revealing the cause-and-effect Pivot that solved it.
